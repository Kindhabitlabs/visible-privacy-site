Keep this site free and independent.

There are no ads here and there never will be. Donations cover the real costs: the research hours behind the Corporate Dossier and the PE Ownership Tracker, and the hosting that keeps them up. Five dollars genuinely helps. So does anything else.

Where your money goes

Mostly research time. Reading filings, checking sources, and keeping the trackers current as companies change hands and the facts move. Some of it is hosting. None of it is advertising, because there isn't any.

The PE Ownership Tracker

The newest thing we're building: a sourced, filterable guide to which everyday businesses private equity has bought, from your vet and dentist to nursing homes, apartments, and local papers. Every entry links to its evidence. Donations fund the research that keeps it accurate as the list grows.

Why no ads

An ad-funded privacy site has to keep advertisers happy, and advertisers are in the same data business this site is about. Staying donation-funded is the only way the guidance stays honest.
